# Texturas generadas - prompts y procedencia

Se generaron cuatro mapas de color con la herramienta integrada de imágenes. No se modificaron sus píxeles al copiarlos al kit. Son propuestas de base color; no un set PBR completo. El pedido de 2048x2048 y seamless dentro de un prompt expresa intención; los metadatos registran el tamaño real y la repetición se revisa en superficies.

## 01_wood_basecolor.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a single square seamless BASE COLOR texture for a stylized low-poly 3D dive-bar game, not concept art and not a rendered material sphere. Generate a flat orthographic wood surface that fills the entire 2048x2048 square edge to edge. Warm medium dark honey-brown wooden boards with simple broad hand-painted grain, a few subtle scratches and dull worn patches, scruffy cheap bar-counter character. Four or five long horizontal boards with thin dark joins, no perspective, no bevel shadows. Make the pattern repeat seamlessly at all four edges: wood grain and board joins must continue left-to-right and top-to-bottom without a border. Low contrast, broad readable forms, no tiny photorealistic noise. Treat this as unlit albedo: uniform lighting, no baked directional highlights, no cast shadow, no ambient occlusion, no specular reflections. No nails, knots that resemble eyes, writing, logos, text, objects, watermark, labels, margins, sheet layout or lighting gradients. Palette brown only, no teal paint. Entire output is the texture itself.

## 02_floor_basecolor.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a single square seamless BASE COLOR floor texture for a stylized low-poly 3D dive-bar game. The whole 2048x2048 image must be the flat texture, viewed precisely straight down with zero perspective. An exactly regular 4 by 4 checkerboard of square ceramic tiles alternating dirty warm cream and muted dark desaturated teal, thin even charcoal-brown grout. Every tile is the exact same size. Broad subtle worn patches and a few shallow stylized scratches, used inexpensive bar floor, no strong unique stains. Match edges for seamless repetition in both directions, grout centered on outer boundaries and the alternating checker pattern continues when repeated. Unlit diffuse color only, uniform light, no ambient occlusion, no directional shadows, no bevel shading, no shine, no light reflections. Hand-painted broad simplified shapes, readable from a game camera, matte and not photorealistic. No objects, furniture, text, logos, labels, borders, watermarks or sheet layout. Entire output is the floor texture itself.

## 03_wall_basecolor.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a single square seamless BASE COLOR plaster wall texture for a scruffy stylized low-poly dive-bar game. Entire 2048x2048 canvas filled with flat smoke-stained warm muted taupe plaster paint, dark dusty beige with restrained broad hand-painted mottling, tiny sparse worn chips, no exposed bricks. No identifiable large stains, no wall trim, no seams, no architecture. Keep the texture low contrast so an animated character stays easy to read in front of it. Edge-to-edge surface with seamless repeat on all four sides, no border. Unlit albedo only: perfectly uniform neutral light, no baked shadows, no highlights, no vignette, no center-to-edge gradient, no specular reflections, no fake 3D relief. Simplified painterly material at game scale, not a photograph or a material ball. No objects, text, logos, labels, signage, watermarks, windows or cracks resembling letters. Entire image is usable surface color.

## 04_fabric_basecolor.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a single square seamless BASE COLOR fabric texture for the burgundy bowling shirt of a comic low-poly adult game character. Entire 2048x2048 image is a flat edge-to-edge swatch of dark muted burgundy cotton fabric, softly faded, with very subtle broad mottling and a restrained regular coarse woven structure. No garment or folds, no seams, no buttons, no collar, no stitched border. Keep the surface matte, low contrast and simplified enough for a stylized indie game, not a macro photograph. Perfectly flat unlit albedo under uniform neutral lighting, no directional shadows, no highlights, no ambient occlusion, no vignette, no lighting gradient. Seamlessly repeatable on every edge. Fabric grain must stay subtle enough not to shimmer at normal gameplay distance. No logos, letters, labels, objects, faces, watermark, sheet layout or skin. Palette deep dusty wine red only. This is texture color data, not a render of clothing.
