# Imágenes entregadas y prompts de referencia

Estado: arte conceptual de preproducción. Generado con la herramienta integrada de imágenes, sin usar API/CLI. Los PNG no son screenshots, modelos 3D, rigs ni texturas listas para motor. Los diagramas 03 a 05 se construyeron con herramientas gráficas deterministas a partir del diseño.

## 01_key_art_concept.png

Prompt utilizado:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original preproduction key art for an indie PC cooperative slapstick game, working title UNCLE EXTRACTION. Create one wide landscape 3D stylized concept painting with an intentionally scruffy low-poly indie game aesthetic, strong readable silhouettes, chunky simplified geometry and matte flat materials, not glossy AAA realism. Scene: inside a shabby fictional American dive bar at closing time, a large middle-aged drunk uncle with a huge dark moustache, burgundy open bowling shirt, faded cream tank top, mustard shorts and worn sandals is being carried horizontally toward the open exit by FOUR distinct adult friends with teal, orange, purple and blue clothing. One holds the feet, one supports the shoulders, one tries to release the uncle's hand gripping a firmly mounted bar counter, the fourth clears away a tipped stool by the exit. The uncle is very alive, belligerent, wide-eyed, comically resisting, with one knee raised preparing a telegraphed kick. A little beer spills; stools wobble. Outside the open doorway, an ordinary parked teal passenger van is visible. Amber warm overhead bar light with cyan neon exit light and deep burgundy shadows. Funny physical predicament, readable action, not cruelty, no blood, no injury, all adults. Camera wide slightly elevated three-quarter cinematic view, prioritize coherent limbs and hand contact. No guns, no superheroes, no supernatural effects, no real brands, no text, no logos, no watermark, no interface. This is concept art, not a screenshot of an existing game.

Uso: dirección visual de escena y composición. Revisar posiciones y contactos al trasladar a una escena real. No tratar la interpretación de una imagen como una regla de colisión.

## 02_uncle_character_reference.png

Se utilizó 01_key_art_concept.png como referencia de identidad y estilo.

Prompt utilizado:

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original character design reference sheet for the mustached adult uncle in the attached reference, preserving his identity: wide huge moustache, broad barrel torso and belly, burgundy open short-sleeve bowling shirt over dirty cream tank top, mustard shorts, worn brown sandals, receding dark hair, thick expressive eyebrows. Asset type: preproduction modeler reference, not an animation sprite sheet and not a finished 3D asset. Wide landscape sheet on clean warm off-white background. Show THREE clear full-body views of the exact same character at consistent scale: front neutral A pose, side neutral A pose, back neutral A pose. At the far right add THREE separated close-up heads with expressions: smug confidence, furious windup, exhausted confused recovery. All full bodies completely inside frame with feet visible. Stylized low-poly 3D matte render matching the attached game's chunky imperfect comic aesthetic. Simple even studio lighting so forms are legible, coherent anatomy and clothing across views. These are approximate conceptual views for a modeler, no claim of perfect orthographic registration. No other characters, no props, no drink, no environment, no lettering, no labels, no logo, no watermark, no gore. Keep layout airy and useful for visual development.

Uso: proporciones y expresiones aproximadas. El modelador resuelve simetría, topología, articulaciones y medidas exactas. No entregar esta imagen como si fuera un turnaround técnico aprobado.

## Continuar el arte sin perder identidad

Usar la lámina del tío como referencia. Especificar función del asset, cámara, escala, materiales y límites. Para una nueva ilustración, conservar ropa, bigote y silueta; para un asset 3D, producir malla, rig, UV y colisiones verificables por separado. Revisar derechos y condiciones de los archivos realmente incluidos antes de publicación.
